### hi, i'm stijn 👋

I'm a software engineering student from the Netherlands, and I like making things all the way
through: the code, but also the icon, the installer, the little sound when something works. Most
of what I build ends up under [Arcnode](https://arcnode.dev), my small studio for my own projects
and the occasional client.

Lately I've been learning to make games, which turned out to be the most fun I've had with a
computer in years.

### what i'm up to

- 🚓 **[Exit Only](https://arcnode.dev/projects/exit-only)**, a police chase through a rainy night
  city that changes every run, with a sliding-car puzzle on the side. Godot, Blender, and a lot of
  late-night playtesting.
- 🎮 **[Arcnode Games](https://arcnode.dev/projects/arcnode-games)**, the little launcher that
  installs and updates it.
- 📓 **[ArcJournal](https://github.com/stinoooo/ArcJournal)**, my journaling app, now being rebuilt
  with end-to-end encryption, for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- ☕ **Learning Java** for school, and how AI actually works under the hood instead of only how to
  use it.
